# Portfolio case-study recommendation

Status: private board-review recommendation only. Nothing in this folder is cleared for public use.

## Recommendation in one page

Feature these three cases in the first private V15 preview:

1. **SCEF: partnership strategy and operating system.** This is the strongest end-to-end consulting story. It moves from an operating problem to a designed framework, a measurement tool, a continuation decision and a practical rollout. It feels senior because the work specifies how a recurring management decision should be made, not just what the client should think.
2. **One Acre Fund: B2B offering selection.** This is the clearest analytical decision story. It exposes the question, the assessment criteria, the comparison and the limitations. It also shows restraint by stating that the recommendation was initial and that the client still owned the decision.
3. **GoodHout: digital growth priorities.** This is the best breadth case for client acquisition. It shows a recognisable commercial problem through a highly legible visual system: customer perception, social deployment, search priorities and a consolidated recommendation. Its evidence chain is thinner than SCEF or OAF, so the captions must not overclaim.

Use these as reserves:

1. **Stahili: funding and stakeholder strategy.** Strong decision matrices and a differentiated stakeholder model. Reserve because the strongest original slides expose client strategy, contain named-client treatment and do not consistently specify owners, timing or success signals.
2. **Circular IQ: circularity positioning.** Excellent visual communication and relevant subject matter. Reserve because only slides 30 and 32 survive the current risk screen without third-party comparative criticism or visible drafting notes. Both are proposals rather than evidenced results.

The three featured cases strengthen the brand in complementary ways. SCEF demonstrates operating-system design. OAF demonstrates transparent analysis and uncertainty. GoodHout demonstrates practical commercial execution. Together they show that the branch can frame a decision, analyse it and translate it into action without inventing an outcome.

## Decision-led card framing

### Card 1

- Service label: `PARTNERSHIP STRATEGY`
- Title: `Turning a scattered partnership approach into a repeatable operating system`
- Context: `An impact organisation needed one consistent way to establish, run, measure and renew partnerships.`
- What was delivered: `A linked set of foundation, operating, measurement and renewal frameworks, plus a phased adoption path.`

### Card 2

- Service label: `MARKET & GROWTH STRATEGY`
- Title: `Choosing which B2B offerings to take forward`
- Context: `An impact organisation needed to compare a broad product set across commercial, operational, impact and risk criteria.`
- What was delivered: `A transparent assessment matrix, explicit limitations and a decision path into the next workstream.`

### Card 3

- Service label: `DIGITAL GROWTH STRATEGY`
- Title: `Prioritising the digital changes that should come first`
- Context: `A circular-materials company needed to align social activity, website content and search visibility around customer needs.`
- What was delivered: `A staged social plan, qualitative website review, prioritised SEO framework and consolidated recommendation.`

These are anonymous public-card drafts. Named-client variants may be used only after the corresponding consent record explicitly approves the name and logo.

## V15 placement plan

Keep the existing three-card row and native dialog interaction. Place SCEF first, OAF second and GoodHout third. This produces the clearest acquisition sequence: operating model, analytical choice, commercial execution. On mobile, preserve the same order.

Inside each dialog, use the original slides in the order listed in `selection-table.md`. Give each image a short caption that identifies the reasoning job it performs. Do not restate it as a claimed result. Keep named source assets outside production build paths until approval. The private preview may use the originals. Production should continue to show the current clean state until every selected case has a case-specific consent record and final visual inspection.

## Reviewer V2 control note

The Reviewer method informed the review but did not approve any slide. The useful controls were quote-or-abstain, decision usefulness over polish, visible comparison, implication and action, a strict finding budget and human sign-off. The current Reviewer evidence base does not license autonomous readiness or publication decisions. No outcome, performance gain, implementation status or client endorsement has been inferred.
