## Approval record fields

- Client approver's name, role and authority to grant permission
- Branch approver and accountable publication owner
- For BCI, Stir It Up and PLAEX, the authorised 180DC signatory and written permission required by the source report
- Exact case title and public URL/placement
- Named or anonymised treatment
- Exact exhibit filenames, source slide/page numbers and SHA-256 hashes
- Client name/logo/product/third-party approvals stated separately
- Exact captions, alt text and surrounding case-card copy
- Geographic scope, duration and withdrawal process for permission
- Any required credit, trademark notice or restriction
- Approval date, evidence location and next review date

## Short client approval-request draft

**Subject: Approval request for a short 180DC portfolio case study**

Hello [name],

We would like to feature a short case study of our work with [client] on the 180 Degrees Consulting Delft-Rotterdam website. The aim is to show the quality of the decision framework and recommendations, not to claim outcomes that have not been verified.

Attached are the exact [number] source slides/pages, captions, alt text and case-card wording we propose to publish. Nothing else from the deliverable would be made public.

Could you please confirm in writing whether you approve:

1. the attached exhibits and wording for public website use;
2. use of the [client name / anonymised description];
3. use of the [logo / no logo] treatment shown;
4. the specific products, locations, figures, recommendations and third-party names visible in the attachments; and
5. our proposed credit and the withdrawal contact/process described below?

Please mark any required change directly in the attached approval sheet. We will not publish the case until every requested change has been applied and the final images have been re-shared for confirmation. Approval can be withdrawn by contacting [branch contact], after which we will remove the case promptly.

Thank you,

[name]  
180 Degrees Consulting Delft-Rotterdam
