# Slide-level selection and evidence register

Status: private preview only. A red finding vetoes publication regardless of score. No selected source has a case-specific consent record in the material reviewed.

## How to read the table

`D/E/A/V/B/S` are 1-5 ratings for decision usefulness, evidence and method, actionability, visual quality, brand value and safety/readiness. The weighted total applies 20/20/15/15/15/15. Classification uses `F` observed in the source, `I` defensible inference, `A` proposed action and `U` unknown or unverified.
