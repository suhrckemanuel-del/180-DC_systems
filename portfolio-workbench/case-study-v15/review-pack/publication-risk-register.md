**Status:** Internal review only. No item below is approved for public release.

**Scope:** the 18 existing gallery assets plus the newly shortlisted Circular IQ, Stir It Up, Viva Independent, SCEF and PLAEX exhibits.

## Decision rule

- **Green:** safe for the private board preview and structurally ready for public use once the named client has approved this exact exhibit, its name/logo treatment and its caption.
- **Amber:** may be used only in a tightly access-controlled private preview. A stated source check, redaction or content-specific approval must be completed before public use.
- **Red:** veto. Do not put the exhibit in a finalist approval preview or public build. The trust problem cannot be removed without materially changing the source exhibit.

Consent is not inferred from possession of the deck, an earlier project relationship or public availability of some facts. No client consent records were found in the reviewed workspace. The BCI, Stir It Up and PLAEX reports also contain an express confidentiality/reproduction clause requiring prior written consent from 180 Degrees Consulting Delft-Rotterdam before any part is communicated to a third party. That internal permission is separate from client consent and must be signed by someone with authority to give it. Consequently, even Green items remain non-public.

## Portfolio-level controls

1. Keep every source and rendered exhibit outside all production build paths until its signed case record is complete.
2. A private preview must be access-controlled, `noindex`, excluded from sitemaps and analytics, and must not expose stable public asset URLs.
3. Strip PDF/PPTX metadata and verify image exports for hidden comments, hyperlinks, QR codes, contact details and speaker notes.
4. Record the source file hash, exact slide/page, approved image hash, approved caption and approval date. Approval of one exhibit does not approve the deck.
5. Do not state recommendations as implemented actions, intended effects as outcomes or old forecasts as current facts.
6. Where a named third party is evaluated, verify facts from primary sources and avoid unnecessary negative judgements. Client approval does not cure defamation or weak sourcing.
7. Any post-consent crop, blur, redaction or caption change requires a second visual review and a new approved-image hash.
8. For BCI, Stir It Up and PLAEX, archive the separate authorised 180DC written permission required by each report's confidentiality/reproduction clause. A project lead's informal approval is insufficient unless that person is authorised to give this permission.

No candidate is public-ready because there is no case-specific consent record. Red exhibits are vetoed even from the finalist approval preview. Green and Amber exhibits may be shown only in the controlled board preview; Amber items must display their unresolved condition next to the exhibit.
