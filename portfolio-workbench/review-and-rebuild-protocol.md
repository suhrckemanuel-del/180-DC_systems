# Review and rebuild protocol

Use this protocol for every shortlisted portfolio candidate. It combines the Quality Reviewer v2 method with the publication-safety gate. It is deliberately stricter than a normal client-deliverable review because the output will be public.

## 1. Create a sanitized source pack

Before any model-assisted review:

- replace the client with `Client A` unless the client has approved naming;
- replace people with roles and remove headshots, emails and contact details;
- remove logos, headers, unusual geographies and details that could re-identify the client;
- remove non-public finance, customer, donor, investor and operational information;
- band any retained figures and state that they are banded;
- retain a private claim register: public claim, source location, approval status and approved wording.

The source pack is local-only and Git-ignored. It must be reviewed by the accountable project or branch lead before it is used.

## 2. Run the Quality Reviewer method

Treat the case study as a **final recommendation deck** or **implementation roadmap**, depending on its structure. Use all ten dimensions:

1. Client decision usefulness
2. Problem framing
3. Storyline and pyramid logic
4. Evidence quality
5. Analysis and insight
6. Recommendation specificity
7. Feasibility and implementation
8. Risks, assumptions and uncertainty
9. Slide-level communication
10. Professionalism, tone and confidentiality

The review follows the normal controls: quote-or-abstain, a maximum of five deep-review findings, an explicit abstention when evidence is missing, and a named human lead who accepts, turns into a question, or rejects each finding. The reviewer advises. The lead decides.

Do not use a readiness level as permission to publish. The current Reviewer v2 trust boundary explicitly prohibits client-facing reliance on the tool alone.

## 3. Rebuild, rather than clean up

The public artifact is a new 5–7 screen story. It should use the source only for approved evidence and exhibit ingredients. It must not be an annotated source deck or a lightly redacted export.

Required components:

- the decision question and concise answer;
- the relevant service area;
- a short, honest explanation of the approach;
- up to three approved insights or actions;
- one or two rebuilt public-safe exhibits;
- limitations where they materially change interpretation; and
- a client-oriented call to action.

## 4. Final public release check

A named branch lead completes `PUBLIC-OK.md` for the candidate, confirming:

- the client naming decision;
- consent or the basis for full anonymisation;
- every approved claim and figure;
- every approved exhibit;
- no unapproved personal, commercial or operational information remains; and
- the public URL and date of release.

No checklist item may be replaced by an assumption.
