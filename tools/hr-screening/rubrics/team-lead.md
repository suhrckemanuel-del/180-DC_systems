# Rubric — Team Lead Track

Used by the comparison agent in every head-to-head match for the **Team Lead** pool. Same five competencies as the Consultant rubric, **reweighted** plus one leadership dimension elevated to the anchor — Team Leads are expected to have *led* people or initiatives, not just contributed. Starting draft; review, adjust, then freeze.

## How the judge uses it
Same mechanics as the Consultant rubric: two blinded **candidate packages (resume + motivation letter)** + this rubric + calibrated anchors → per-dimension 1–4 scores, a `winner`, `confidence`, and an evidence-citing rationale. Resume → TL1/TL2/TL4/TL5 (what they led/did); motivation letter → **TL6 (mission fit)** and a writing sample for **TL3 (communication)**.

**Scale (1–4, no midpoint):** 4 = clear, specific, outcome-linked · 3 = adequate but vague · 2 = thin/implied · 1 = none or contradicting.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Weight | What "4" looks like on a resume |
|---|-----------|--------|----------------------------------|
| TL1 | **Analytical / Problem-Solving** | **20%** | Framed the problem, not just solved a sub-task. *e.g. "Structured a market-sizing analysis for a 5-person team; led synthesis to the final recommendation."* |
| TL2 | **Leadership / Team Direction** | **30%** | Held accountability for others' output; drove a group to a goal; managed up or across. *e.g. "Led a 6-person team through a 10-week engagement — allocated tasks, ran weekly syncs, delivered the final deck to the CEO."* (2 = "was part of leadership team"; 1 = no evidence of directing others.) |
| TL3 | **Communication / Structured Thinking** | **15%** | As Consultant C2, weighted toward stakeholder-facing output (board reports, client-facing briefs). |
| TL4 | **Initiative / Ownership** | **15%** | Ownership that extended to others — "made sure my team had what they needed," not just personal initiative. |
| TL5 | **Cross-Functional Impact** | **10%** | Worked across teams/orgs, coordinated competing interests, navigated stakeholders. |
| TL6 | **Motivation / Mission Fit** | **10%** | *(Mainly from the motivation letter.)* As Consultant C5, plus evidence of building culture, recruiting others to a cause, or sustained commitment over time. |

**Total: 100%.**

**The defining difference from the Consultant rubric:** Leadership/Team Direction (TL2) at 30% is the anchor. A candidate strong on analysis but with **no evidence of directing others** should not outrank a candidate who has both — regardless of pedigree. The verification pass specifically flags any top-ranked Team Lead applicant with no TL2 evidence as a misranked Consultant.

## Hard instructions to the judge (anti-bias)
Identical to the Consultant rubric: ignore school/employer prestige and GPA; do not penalize non-business major, non-target school, unstated GPA, or limited professional experience; reward depth over breadth and outcomes over keywords; require specific evidence for motivation.

## Anchor examples (fill before freezing)
Provide 3–5 verdict-balanced worked pairs, at least one of which turns on **leadership evidence** specifically (so the judge learns TL2 is the differentiator). Hand-labelled by chapter leaders; locked with the rubric.
