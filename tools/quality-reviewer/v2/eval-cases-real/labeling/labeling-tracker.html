How we work through this

14 real anonymised cases. Two people label each one blind and independently, then the two of them adjudicate it into a gold file. The gold is only as good as the discipline.

  1. Everyone reads the rubric once, cold. 01-rubric-v1.md and 05-eval-harness.md section C, before your first case.
  2. Two labelers per case, working alone. Assign two names to each case below. Each opens the case in labeling-workstation.html, fills the worksheet, downloads it. Do not discuss a case until both worksheets for it exist.
  3. Do the four calibration cases first. Both assigned labelers do all four before anyone touches the other ten. This is where we find out if the rubric carries two readers to the same answer.
  4. Adjudicate the batch together. Open both worksheets in adjudication-workstation.html. It computes the readiness agreement, you resolve any gap on the merits, it writes the gold file. Record the result on this tracker.
  5. Check the stop rule. If two or more of the four batch cases land two levels apart or contested, stop and fix the rubric before the other ten. Otherwise split the remaining ten and go.
  6. Gold files land in eval-cases-real/. Keep both worksheets, they are the audit trail. Fill agreement-log.md as you go.
Blindness rules, non-negotiable. Do not open the sealed selection memo. Do not read any AI review or triage band for these cases. Do not show the other labeler your worksheet before both exist. If you accidentally see any of that for a case, say so in the worksheet header for that case.
If more than two people label: the protocol was written for two labelers. Using a larger pool is fine, but two different people labeling two different halves of the set adds a consistency risk the agreement log cannot see. Keep pairs stable, and have one case labeled by everyone as a cross-check if you can.
