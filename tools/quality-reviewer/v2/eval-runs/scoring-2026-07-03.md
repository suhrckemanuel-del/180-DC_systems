## 1. Method note

I am a fresh-context independent scorer. I did not build this tool and I have no stake in whether it passes. I read only the harness (05-eval-harness.md) and for each of the five cases the deck, the gold label and the live AI output JSON listed in the task. The specific outputs I scored were case-01-review-v2-live.json, case-02-review-v2-live-r2.json, case-03-review-v2-live.json, case-04-review-v2-live-r2.json and case-05-review-v2-live.json. I did not read progress.md, the sprint report, the prior scoring file, the decision log, the prompt templates or the rubric file. I did not see how the prompt was built or any earlier scoring. All judgments below are my own read of the decks against the golds.

Scoring convention. A true positive (TP) is an AI finding or comment that maps to a gold real issue or acceptable feedback. A false positive (FP) is a flagged issue that is wrong for the case or that the gold names as a false-positive-to-avoid or unacceptable. A low-value note is a comment that is defensible but does not earn its place in the noise budget. Recall denominator is the count of gold serious issues (the ranked real top issues). Findings and top-level comments are classified. Scorecard dimensions, strengths, questions and timeline are context not scored as findings.

## 5. Case 05 adjudication

The gold expects R3 with at most two minor findings and names inventing criticals as the failure mode. The AI produced R2 with three findings all labelled major, two of them deliveryCritical true. My independent verdict, finding by finding.

Finding 1, gate below assumption. The underlying logic is genuinely sharp and I credit it as a real catch a good senior reviewer might make. Slide 8 rests the 5,000 pound case on 8% of the full 1,400 list. Slide 9 runs the pilot on the most engaged 200, who by construction convert above the list average. Slide 10 greenlights full launch at 5% on that friendly segment. So passing the gate does not validate the 8% the revenue rests on. That is a true internal inconsistency in the test design. However it is not delivery-critical and it is not a blocking issue. The deck already flags 8% as the main thing to test and builds a soft launch precisely to de-risk it. The reviewer is refining a test the team already got mostly right. That is a minor note, not a major delivery-critical finding. The content is genuine, the severity is inflated.

Finding 2, materiality unsized. Weaker. The client question is should Client E introduce a paid membership to diversify income and if so how. The deck answers yes, starts with one tier, sizes a conservative year-one figure and marks it as the thing to test. Noting that 5,000 is never divided by total income is fair polish, but it is not a major gap. A deliberately small first-year pilot does not have to prove full materiality on slide 8, that is what the scale-up decision after the soft launch is for. Escalating this to a major delivery-critical finding is the padding the case is built to catch. This is over-escalation.

Finding 3, overstated benchmark. Correct content, wrong severity. It matches the gold minor issue 2 about comparability and the misuse of the 32% attendance rate as a proxy for willingness to pay. As a minor note it is exactly right. Scored major, it inflates.

Overall verdict. The gold is not too lenient. None of the three findings is a genuine senior-consultant critical that the gold missed. One is a sharp minor, one is a fair minor, one is soft polish dressed as major. The reviewer found real texture but then over-escalated all of it to major and let two self-assigned delivery-criticals drag readiness from R3 down to R2. That is precisely the manufactured over-escalation this case tests for. My call: the reviewer inflated severity and needs calibration. Gold stays at R3. The reviewer fails the restraint test on case 05.

One point in the reviewer's favor for the record. It did not invent a fabricated fact, it did not flag the 8% as simply unsourced (it engaged with the assumption honestly), it did not attack the single-tier choice as unambitious, and its strengths section is genuine and strengths-first. So it avoided the crudest failure modes. But avoiding the crudest form does not clear the bar. Three majors and a demotion to R2 is a fail against gold and against harness section E point 4.
