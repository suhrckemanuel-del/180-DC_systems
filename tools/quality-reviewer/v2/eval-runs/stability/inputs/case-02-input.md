**Slide 1. Title**
Client B growth plan. Closing the funding gap.

**Slide 2. The problem**
Client B faces a 25% funding gap next year. Individual donations have been flat for
three years. The board has asked how to grow individual giving.

**Slide 3. Donation landscape**
Most of Client B's income is grants. Individual donations are a small share and come
mostly from a one-off annual event. There is no regular-giving programme.

**Slide 4. What donors say**
In a short survey, donors said they value Client B's tutoring results and would give
more if they saw impact updates.

**Slide 5. Programme review**
Client B runs three programmes: after-school tutoring, holiday camps and a mentoring
scheme. The mentoring scheme has the weakest attendance.

**Slide 6. Programme performance**
Tutoring shows the strongest outcomes. Camps break even. Mentoring runs at a loss and
attendance is falling.

**Slide 7. Our view**
Client B should cut the mentoring scheme and redirect its budget to tutoring, which
delivers the best outcomes per pound.

**Slide 8. Recommendation**
Close the mentoring scheme. Reinvest the freed budget in tutoring. This improves
overall programme efficiency.

**Slide 9. Next steps**
Plan the mentoring wind-down. Communicate to families. Reallocate staff to tutoring.
