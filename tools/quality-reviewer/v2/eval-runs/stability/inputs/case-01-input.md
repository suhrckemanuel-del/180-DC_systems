**Slide 3. About Client A**
Client A redistributes surplus food to 40 community partners across Region A. It
operates one warehouse and two vans. It relies on volunteer drivers and a single
full-time coordinator.

**Slide 4. Current operations**
In the last year Client A moved 620 tonnes of food. Partner demand in Region A
frequently exceeds supply. Volunteers report turning away partners in peak months.

**Slide 5. The opportunity in Region B**
Region B has 30 potential partners and no comparable redistributor. Early conversations
suggest strong interest. Region B demand will grow 40% over the next three years.

**Slide 6. Option one. Deepen Region A**
Add a second warehouse shift and recruit more volunteer drivers to meet unmet demand in
the current region.

**Slide 7. Option two. Expand to Region B**
Open a satellite hub in Region B to serve the 30 new partners and capture the growing
demand there.

**Slide 8. Our view**
Region B is the larger prize. The unmet demand in Region A is real but bounded, while
Region B offers a step change in reach. Client A should prioritize Region B.

**Slide 9. Recommendation**
Expand to Region B. Scale up operations and improve partnerships to serve the new
region. Build on Client A's strong volunteer base.

**Slide 10. Next steps**
Begin planning the Region B expansion. Engage stakeholders. Review progress in due
course.
