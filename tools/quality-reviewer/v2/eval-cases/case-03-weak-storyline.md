# Case 03. Weak storyline, no governing insight

Synthetic and fully anonymized. Built to test whether the reviewer catches a findings
dump that never answers the client's question, with topic-label titles and no governing
insight.

- **Client (anonymized):** Client C, a community health clinic.
- **Artifact type:** draft deck.
- **Client question:** Which of three possible new services should Client C launch first
  with its limited capacity.
- **Draft maturity:** working draft, 10 slides.

---

**Slide 1. Title**
Client C service expansion. Research findings.

**Slide 2. Agenda**
Background. Methodology. Findings. Appendix.

**Slide 3. Background**
Client C serves 2,000 patients a year. It has capacity to add one new service. Three
options are on the table: mental-health counselling, physiotherapy and a diabetes
programme.

**Slide 4. Methodology**
We interviewed staff, surveyed 120 patients and reviewed local health data.

**Slide 5. Finding: mental health**
Patients report long waits for mental-health support elsewhere. Staff see demand.

**Slide 6. Finding: physiotherapy**
Physiotherapy referrals often go unfilled locally. Equipment cost is high.

**Slide 7. Finding: diabetes**
Local diabetes rates are above the regional average. A programme would need a specialist
nurse.

**Slide 8. Patient survey results**
40% of surveyed patients said mental health was their top unmet need, 30% said diabetes,
20% physiotherapy, 10% other.

**Slide 9. Considerations**
Each option has benefits and costs. Capacity, funding and staffing all matter. Further
analysis may be useful.

**Slide 10. Thank you**
Questions welcome.
