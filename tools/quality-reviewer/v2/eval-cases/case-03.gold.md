# Case 03. Gold label

Human-written before the AI reviewed the case. Voice rules apply.

- **Case:** [case-03-weak-storyline.md](case-03-weak-storyline.md)
- **Artifact type:** draft deck.
- **Expected readiness:** Needs substantial revision (R1).
- **Expected readiness reason:** the deck presents findings but never answers which
  service to launch first, so it does not answer the client's question.

## Real top issues (ranked)

1. **No governing insight, the question is unanswered (blocking, rule 2, dimensions 1
   and 3).** The client asked which service to launch first. Slide 9 concludes only that
   each option has benefits and costs and "further analysis may be useful". No
   recommendation is made. Ceiling R1. Severity: high. Issue type: thinking.
2. **The evidence points to an answer the deck does not draw (major, dimension 5).**
   Slide 8 shows mental health is the top unmet need at 40%, matched by the demand
   signal on slide 5. The deck describes this and stops rather than drawing the
   implication. Severity: high. Issue type: thinking.
3. **Titles are topic labels (major, dimension 9).** Agenda, Background, Methodology,
   Considerations. A reader cannot follow an argument from the titles because there is no
   argument in them. Severity: medium. Issue type: communication.

## Acceptable AI feedback

- Names the absence of a recommendation as the central problem, not a polish issue.
- Notes that the survey and demand evidence already point toward mental health, and asks
  the team to draw the implication rather than drawing it for them.
- Flags the topic-label titles.
- Readiness R1.

## Unacceptable AI feedback

- Deciding for the team that mental health is the answer and writing that recommendation.
  The reviewer surfaces that the evidence points somewhere and asks the team to commit,
  it does not make the call.
- Inventing capacity or cost numbers.

## False positives to avoid

- Flagging the methodology as weak. It is thin but adequate for the stage. The problem is
  the missing so-what, not the method.
- Flagging slide 10 (Thank you) as a content failure.

## False negatives to catch

- Missing that there is no recommendation at all. A review that treats this as a
  near-ready deck needing tidier titles has failed.
