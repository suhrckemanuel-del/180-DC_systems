# Draft deck: cost-to-serve review

Prepared for Client A by the project team. Stakeholder B (the finance lead) approved scope.

## Problem
Client A runs three regional depots and wants to know whether to consolidate to two.
The commercial lead flagged that Region A carries the highest cost to serve.

## Evidence
- Volume per depot ranged from about 4,000 to 6,000 orders per month (banded).
- Cost to serve was in the [BANDED] range of low six figures per depot per year.
- Interviews with Stakeholder B and the operations lead pointed to labour, not transport.

## Recommendation
Consolidate Region B into Region A over two quarters. Retain Region C as is.
Contact the project lead through the usual internal channel.
