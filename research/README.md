# Research

The evidence base behind the methodology. Every design choice in the tools traces back to one of these angles. Claims here were checked: see [verification-report.md](verification-report.md) for what verified, what was partial and what failed (some widely-quoted stats did not hold up and are flagged do-not-cite).

## The angles

| Angle | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [01 Deliverable quality anatomy](01-deliverable-quality-anatomy/findings.md) | What makes a consulting deliverable good: the quality dimensions and the ERIP model. |
| [02 Failure modes in student consulting](02-failure-modes-student-consulting/findings.md) | The recurring ways student decks fail. Missing governing insight and non-actionable recommendations lead. |
| [04 Quality assurance in professional services](04-quality-assurance-professional-services/findings.md) | How MBB firms actually QA work: horizontal flow test, storyboard reviews. |
