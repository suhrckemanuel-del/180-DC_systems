# Erasmusbrug hero set

Five coordinated 1536 × 1024 PNG hero backgrounds generated with the built-in
image-generation tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: premium website hero background in a coordinated five-image
Erasmusbrug series  
Scene: the Erasmusbrug spanning the Nieuwe Maas in Rotterdam, seen from a broad
riverbank viewpoint; preserve its recognizable asymmetrical white pylon,
harp-like cable geometry, and long horizontal deck. Keep the distant skyline
subdued and generic, with no legible signs or branded buildings.  
Style: atmospheric cinematic architecture with photographic foundations and
restrained painterly texture; a sophisticated editorial matte-painting finish,
with natural materials and plausible light rather than flat documentary
photography.  
Composition: wide 3:2 landscape. Bridge and pylon occupy the middle and upper
thirds. Keep the entire lower third calm, low-detail, and usable for a text
overlay.  
Continuity: elegant, restrained, European, and realistic, with consistent visual
sophistication across the set.  
Constraints: no people, readable text, typography, logos, watermarks, obvious
brand marks, borders, split screens, collages, storm damage, danger, or fantasy
architecture.

## Variant modifiers

### 01 — Blue Hour

Moody blue-hour dusk with soft river mist and long-exposure traffic light trails
along the bridge deck. Quiet, cinematic, and slightly mysterious; diffuse
ambient light and subtle bridge illumination softened by mist. Restrained navy,
slate, charcoal blue, and muted cool gray. Smooth dark misty water in the lower
third. Avoid sunset warmth, bright cyan, neon spectacle, crisp midday
visibility, and busy boats.

### 02 — Golden Hour Drama

The bridge strongly backlit by a very low setting sun. The pylon and cables form
a sharp silhouette against an amber-to-deep-orange sky, with the river
reflecting the color. Bold but refined golden-hour drama, luminous haze, warm
amber, burnished gold, deep orange, and dark graphite silhouettes. Keep the
lower third in near-silhouette shadow with broad, subdued bands of reflected
warmth. Avoid pastel pink, a cheerful postcard look, flare clutter, and blown-out
bridge geometry.

### 03 — Storm Light

A dramatic breaking-storm sky above the intact bridge. Massive dark cloud layers
part for one shaft of sunlight to reach the bridge and river. Dark, choppy water
in the middle distance. High-contrast cinematic energy with no damage, danger,
lightning, flooding, or emergency activity. Mostly desaturated charcoal, steel
gray, and cold slate, with pale gold confined to the light shaft. Keep the lower
third broad-toned and low-detail; no debris, boats, tornadoes, lightning bolts,
or apocalyptic color.

### 04 — Minimalist Fog

Extremely dense white fog swallows nearly the entire scene. Only the upper pylon
and a few upper cable lines emerge faintly before dissolving back into mist.
Everything below mid-frame is a seamless, near-white void. Hushed,
contemplative, almost monochrome quiet luxury in pearl white, pale warm gray, and
faint cool silver. No skyline, water texture, hard lower edges, dramatic clouds,
or colored light.

### 05 — Night Energy

Deep night with the bridge elegantly lit by architectural lighting.
Long-exposure traffic trails sweep across the deck while sky and water remain
near-black. Near-black, ink navy, and clean architectural white, with only tiny
naturally occurring green-adjacent navigation or signal lights. The lower third
is broad, dark water with sparse soft reflections. Avoid cyberpunk neon,
oversaturated green, colored floodlighting, fireworks, star trails, and busy
signage.
