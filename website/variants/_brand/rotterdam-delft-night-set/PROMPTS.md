# Rotterdam–Delft night set

Three coordinated 1536 × 1024 PNG hero backgrounds generated with the built-in
image-generation tool.

Style reference:
`../erasmusbrug-hero-set/05-night-energy.png`

The reference was used only for palette, exposure, long-exposure language, and
finish—not for subject geometry or viewpoint.

## Shared direction

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: premium website hero background in a coordinated Rotterdam–Delft
night series  
Style: atmospheric long-exposure architectural photography with subtle
painterly editorial finishing, realistic materials, and accurate landmark
geometry.  
Composition: wide cinematic 3:2 landscape. Keep the bottom third extremely dark,
uncluttered, and low-detail for text overlay.  
Lighting: quiet, sophisticated, high-contrast European night; near-black sky and
water, controlled highlights, cool architectural whites, and sparse warm or red
accents.  
Constraints: no people, readable text, typography, logos, watermarks, borders,
collages, daylight, blue-hour sky, or cyberpunk neon.

## Rotterdam #2 — Erasmusbrug pylon

Erasmusbrug at night from close to the base of its asymmetrical pylon, using a
low oblique viewpoint that looks upward along the dramatic cable fan. The white
steel catches cool architectural floodlighting against a near-black sky. The
deck sweeps across the lower-middle frame with continuous long-exposure red
traffic trails. Deep Nieuwe Maas water fills the lower third with minimal soft
reflections. Preserve recognizable pylon proportions and harp-like cable
geometry.

## Delft #1 — Oostpoort

The Oostpoort gate and Oostpoortbrug at night with accurate, intimate Dutch
Gothic architecture: paired slender towers, steep roofs, and the brick gatehouse
beside the narrow canal. Warm interior window light glows against a near-black
sky. A faint traffic trail appears only on the adjacent street. Still canal
water occupies much of the lower third with soft reflections. Keep the scale
quiet and historic, avoiding festive lighting, postcard brightness, fantasy
castle details, or modern high-rises.

## Delft #2 — canal

A classic narrow Delft canal at night, lined with trees and authentic historic
brick facades. One or two warm streetlamps are the only strong light sources.
Long exposure renders the water still and the lamp reflections soft. Buildings
form a modest middle band while dark sky and canal occupy most of the frame.
Nearly monochrome near-black, ink navy, charcoal, and muted brick with very
limited amber light. Avoid people, boats, bicycles, cars, bright shops, readable
signs, saturated color, and tourist-postcard treatment.
