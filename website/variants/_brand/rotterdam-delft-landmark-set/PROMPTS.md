# Rotterdam–Delft landmark set

Four coordinated 1536 × 1024 PNG hero backgrounds generated with the built-in
image-generation tool.

## Shared direction

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: premium website hero background in a coordinated Rotterdam–Delft
architectural campaign  
Style: cinematic architectural photography with realistic landmark geometry,
natural materials, and a restrained painterly editorial finish.  
Composition: wide cinematic 3:2 landscape with clean visual hierarchy and calm
negative space.  
Constraints: no people, crowds, readable text, typography, signs, house numbers,
logos, banners, advertisements, watermarks, borders, collages, fantasy
architecture, construction cranes, or tourist clutter.

## 03 — Markthal, evening

Rotterdam's Markthal at dusk, seen from the open plaza. Show the huge
horseshoe-shaped archway at dramatic scale, with its full curved opening and
glazed end facade. Warm market illumination glows inside while the exterior grey
cladding stays cool against a deepening blue sky. Use a slightly low, broad
viewpoint; keep the upper sky simple for navigation and the foreground plaza
spare. Avoid shoppers, stalls, storefront text, billboards, festival lighting,
and bright cars.

## 04 — Cube Houses, late afternoon

Rotterdam's Kubuswoningen in warm late-afternoon light, viewed from a clean low
angle. Emphasize the authentic repeating tilted yellow cube geometry, dark
window grids, and rhythmic diagonal faces against a soft blue sky. Warm sunlight
grazes the yellow planes while shadows remain cool. Keep the treatment graphic
and playful but still refined and photographic. Avoid pedestrians, bicycles,
cars, signs, fisheye distortion, tourist clutter, warped architecture, and
cartoon color.

## 05 — Depot Boijmans, golden hour

Depot Boijmans Van Beuningen at golden hour, showing the full sculptural
mirrored bowl silhouette. Its rounded reflective skin catches warm sky tones and
softly distorted Museumpark reflections. Keep the result luminous, serene,
dreamlike, and photographic, with generous clean sky and a simple park
foreground for text. Avoid people, bicycles, cars, foreground sculptures,
melting architecture, blown highlights, dense trees, logos, and reflected
lettering.

## 06 — Delft Markt and Nieuwe Kerk, golden hour

Delft's calm Markt square at golden hour, looking toward the recognizable Nieuwe
Kerk tower above historic brick and stone rooflines. Warm low sunlight touches
the facades and masonry under a softly colored sky. Keep the square uncluttered,
the facades as a balanced middle band, and the upper sky clean for navigation.
Avoid market stalls, tents, people, bicycles, cars, cafe terraces, readable
signage, modern street clutter, postcard saturation, and fantasy church details.
