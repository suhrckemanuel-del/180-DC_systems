01The two roles

Arrive fresh, or lead a team through a cycle

Consultant

No experience required — and we mean it. Arrive fresh and learn on the job, with a trained team and a Team Leader around you. You'll work directly with a real organisation's leadership for a cycle and leave with a board-ready deliverable you helped build.

Team Leader

Team Leaders lead a consulting team through a cycle. Experience is wanted here: the usual path is one cycle as a Consultant, then stepping up to Team Leader the next. You'll own scope, client relationship, and quality for your project.

02What you learn, who we want

What you learn: structured problem-solving, client communication, and AI-assisted consulting methods no other student club teaches.

Who we want: students from both campuses — TU Delft technical depth and Erasmus business acumen are equally the brand.
