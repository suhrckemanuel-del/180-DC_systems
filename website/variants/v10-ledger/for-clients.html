Client account — terms

A consulting team, at no cost, on your hardest question.

If your organisation's primary goal is impact rather than profit — a non-profit, a social enterprise, a mission-driven institution — you likely qualify[F1]. Student consultants do it for the experience and the mission; quality is protected by our review system[F2], not a fee. These are the full terms, posted openly.

Postings — the engagement, week by week

Ten to twelve weeks, four postings, an exit at every gate

WK 00

Scoping call

A structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like. One hour, no commitment — nothing is posted to the account until you say so.

WK 01

Proposal, signed by you

A written scope: what we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end. No surprises mid-project — scope changes are re-signed, never assumed.

WK 02–11

The project

A team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated team lead[F3]. A mid-point review where you course-correct, working sessions as the analysis firms up. Your time: about an hour a week.

FINAL WK

Board-ready presentation

Presented to you and yours, every underlying analysis handed over. Yours to use, cite, and build on — the account closes with you holding everything.

Assurance — why free doesn't mean unchecked

Audit us before you trust us

Every deliverable passes an AI-assisted senior review before it reaches you — built by our branch, published in the open[F2], checking evidence, logic, and clarity against professional consulting standards. Its rule is this site's rule: a finding must quote the document verbatim, or stay silent.

And the honest limits, on the record: we are students; engagements take a semester; we decline scopes we can't do well. The "no" is part of the quality system.
