Katern 2 · the lead

Your hardest question, taken on for a semester — here is exactly how

If your organisation's primary goal is impact rather than profit, you likely qualify. The terms below are the whole of it; there is no fine print because there is no fee.

Assignments desk · Delft & Rotterdam

An engagement begins with an hour and ends with a board. The scoping call surveys your challenge, your constraints, and what success looks like; the written proposal that follows is signed by you before any work begins — what we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end.

Then a team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated lead works the question for eight to ten weeks, with a mid-point review where you course-correct. Your time: about an hour a week. The final week delivers a board-ready presentation, every underlying analysis handed over — yours to use, cite, and build on.
