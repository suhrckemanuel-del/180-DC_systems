The thesis

V8 — De Courant

Thesis: a Dutch broadsheet front page — blackletter masthead, column rules, decks and bylines, recruitment as classified ads, and the branch's honesty rules printed as an actual editorial colophon ("what this paper will not print"). The newspaper is the civic-trust register: it frames pro bono consulting as public-interest work, and gives quote-or-abstain its natural home — journalism's own rulebook.

Content identical across all ten variants (see website/variants/content.md); this variant's twist is the *voice* — every section rewritten as news copy, which the honesty rules make possible: news voice only works when every sentence is checkable, and here it is.

The system

Specification

1Palettenewsprint #F7F4EC · ink #191713 · 180-green #00693C/#00512E reserved for the paper's own "brand red" role (kickers, prices, key figures) · amber only in awaiting-copy slots. Duotone discipline: a newspaper has ink and one accent.
2TypeUnifrakturCook (the blackletter masthead — 17 KB, used for masthead and drop caps only) + Newsreader 400/600/800 with true italics for decks and op-ed voice. Column rules at 1px, section rules at 1.5px, double rules frame the page.
3Formslead story with deck + byline + three-column body and drop cap · fact box ("de koersen") with an honestly empty row · classifieds (GEVRAAGD / AANGEBODEN) for join and partners · the team as redactie list · the colophon block in solid ink listing what will never be printed · engagement as a dienstregeling timetable on Katern 2.
4Motionnone, and zero JavaScript. Newsprint does not move; hover states are underlines and ink inversions. The most JS-free variant alongside Pamflet.
5Honestythe colophon IS the honesty system, printed as editorial policy; gaps are "awaiting copy" slots in editor's italic; the op-ed argues the practice ("an empty column you can trust…").
Method & extension

How to print the next edition

Build: hand-written static HTML/CSS, one stylesheet, no framework, no JavaScript, no images — the masthead is type, the texture is rules and columns. ~212 KB of self-hosted woff2.

To extend: Our Work becomes the archive — one case per article with a real dateline; Impact is the year-end numbers page (printed only when the numbers exist); events run as announcements. Rules: every sentence must survive a fact-check; the colophon appears on every page; green never exceeds its accent role; if a section can't be written as news, it isn't ready to print.

Deploy: consolidated Cloudflare Pages tree via tools/deploy-cf.ps1.
