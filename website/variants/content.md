## 3. Service areas (the six, verbatim names from the live page, one-line descriptions written for clients)

1. **Digital Transformation** — putting the right tools and processes in place so a small team stops losing hours to manual work.
2. **Marketing & Engagement** — reaching the audiences your mission serves, and the donors and volunteers who sustain it.
3. **Financial Sustainability** — building funding models and cost structures that survive beyond the next grant cycle.
4. **Market Assessment** — evidence on where demand, partners, and competition actually are before you commit resources.
5. **Operational Efficiency** — finding where effort leaks out of your processes and redesigning them around your capacity.
6. **Impact Measurement** — defining and tracking the numbers that prove your work works, for boards and funders.

## 4. The engagement (For Clients spine)

- **Who qualifies:** non-profits, social enterprises, and mission-driven organisations. If your primary goal is impact rather than profit, you likely qualify.
- **What it costs:** **Nothing. Our work is pro bono.** Say it early and big. Student consultants do it for the experience and the mission; quality is protected by our review system, not a fee.
- **The timeline (visualise in every variant):**
  1. **Scoping call** (week 0) — a structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like.
  2. **Proposal** (week 1) — a written scope you sign off on. No surprises mid-project.
  3. **The project** (8–10 weeks) — a team of 4–6 student consultants, a dedicated team lead, and named client touchpoints: a mid-point review where you course-correct, and working sessions as the analysis firms up.
  4. **Final presentation** (last week) — a board-ready deck, presented to you, with everything handed over.
- **The quality story (real, current):** every deliverable gets a senior-reviewer treatment before it reaches the client — an AI-assisted review system our branch builds in-house and publishes in the open, checking evidence, logic, and clarity against professional consulting standards. We are the only student consultancy we know of that can show you its quality system: [github.com/suhrckemanuel-del/180-DC_systems](https://github.com/suhrckemanuel-del/180-DC_systems).
