Why the work holds up

Every deliverable passes an inspectable review before a client sees it

Student consultancies claim quality; we can show ours. Every deck gets a senior-reviewer treatment — an AI-assisted review our branch builds in-house and publishes in the open — checking evidence, logic, and clarity against professional consulting standards.

The rule underneath it all: a finding must quote the document verbatim, or stay silent. The consultant does every rewrite; the reviewer only teaches.

Reviewed — evidence checked

This deck plays by its own rules: no invented statistics, no fake logos, sources on every slide — because that's what "board-ready" means.

first published case study — in preparation with client consent; the frame only ever holds real work
