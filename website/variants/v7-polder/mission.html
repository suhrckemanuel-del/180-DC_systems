Part of 180 Degrees Consulting worldwide

Delft–Rotterdam is one branch of 180 Degrees Consulting, a student-run university-based consultancy network with branches at universities around the world. Every branch is independently run by its own students, on the same mission: high-quality strategy consulting, made uniquely affordable for the organisations doing good and least able to pay for it. No single branch keeps its own feet dry alone.
