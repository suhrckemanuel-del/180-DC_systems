NAP −1.00 m — the reclamation, stage by stage · 10–12 weeks

How land gets made

WK 0 · survey

Scoping call

A structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like. One hour, no commitment — no dike gets built before the survey.

WK 1 · the dike

Proposal, signed by you

A written scope: what we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end. The dike line is drawn together, then it holds.

WKS 2–11 · pumping

The project

A team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated team lead. A mid-point review where you course-correct, working sessions as the analysis firms up. Your time: about an hour a week.

FINAL WK · dry land

Board-ready presentation

Presented to you and yours, every underlying analysis handed over. Yours to build on — reclaimed land belongs to the people who live on it.

NAP −2.00 m — why free doesn't mean unchecked

The pump runs on every deliverable

Before any deck reaches you it passes an AI-assisted senior review our branch builds and publishes in the open — evidence, logic, and clarity checked against professional consulting standards. Its rule: a finding must quote the document verbatim, or stay silent. Inspect the pump; it's public.

The honest limits, surveyed and marked: we are students; engagements take a semester; we decline scopes we can't do well. Saying "no" is how the water stays out.
