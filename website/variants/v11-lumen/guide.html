Lumen.

Design specification — not client-facing.

Thesis

A close homage to the Imperial College Union template

This variant deliberately mirrors the structure of Imperial College Union's activity-page template (their consultancy society page was the direct reference): full-width flat colour-block sections, a giant display title with a metadata block in the hero, an "interested in joining?" callout with a pill button, and the signature committee list of huge names.

Recoloured to a white-dominant palette — ICU's loud orange and pink blocks become near-white green tints, and the footer keeps a deep green (ICU's own footer is green, and it happens to be exactly on-brand for us). The verified 180DC green #78B038 is the single accent. Our content throughout; born already-correct on the 2026-07-23 rebrand spec.

Design system

Specification

Palette

Paper #FFFFFF, faint green block #F3F8EC and deeper green block #E7F1D6 in place of ICU's orange and pink, callout #EAF3DF in place of its lilac. Ink #14180F, secondary #55604B. The verified 180DC green #78B038 is the only accent (links drop to AA-safe #4A7322); the footer is a deep on-brand green #23431C.

Type

Heavy grotesque display via a system stack (Helvetica Neue / Arial / Segoe UI) at 800 weight, tight −0.03em, uppercase — the giant hero title and committee names read like ICU's. Body in system-ui. No web fonts, zero asset weight.

Layout

Full-bleed flat colour-block sections stacked top to bottom, 1240px inner measure. Hairline #DDE4D2 rules. No radii except the pill buttons and social dots. Breadcrumb on every page.

ICU devices, mirrored

The giant display hero with a right-aligned metadata block (Founded / Cities / Network / Cost + a quality-system link + social dots); the "interested in joining?" callout with a pill button; the "products"-style colour block for services; and the committee list rendered as huge names with small role labels, separated by rules.

Method & honesty

How it was made & how to extend it

Build: hand-written static HTML/CSS, one stylesheet, no framework, no build step, no web fonts. The only assets are three compressed WebP logo files shared across the golden-set variants. One JS-gated, reduced-motion-gated fade-in; everything renders without JavaScript.

Born correct on the rebrand spec: built after the 2026-07-23 client feedback round, so it starts already-correct — the verified brand green (never the invented pine-green), the real logo and a green favicon, the affordable-not-free terminology, Project Manager / Branch President roles, the Mission and Our Work sections, the two real consulting cycles (Oct–Jan, Mar–Jun) and recruitment windows (Sept, Jan/Feb), and every honest placeholder. Content source: website/variants/content-v2v3v7.md.

Honesty devices: every gap is a visible dashed-green placeholder — impact facts, case studies (with the Situation → Approach → Outcome structure shown), team photos, the roster (flagged pending Monday.com confirmation), recruitment portal, events, testimonials, partner logos, and the non-submitting form. Nothing is invented.

To extend: new pages stack more .block colour sections; reuse .card, .callout, .committee, and the metadata hero. Keep the one-accent rule and ICU's flat-block discipline — no gradients, no shadows.
