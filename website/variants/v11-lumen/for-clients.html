The engagement — no surprises

How an engagement works

We run two consulting cycles a year: October–January and March–June. Each engagement is scoped to a single cycle — roughly a semester of work by a dedicated team, with a written scope you sign off on and named touchpoints along the way.

Start of cycle

Scoping call

A structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like. One hour, no commitment.

Week 1

Proposal, signed by you

A written scope: what we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end. No surprises mid-project.

Through the cycle

The project

A team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated Project Manager. A mid-point review where you course-correct, and working sessions as the analysis firms up. Your time: about an hour a week.

End of cycle

Board-ready presentation

Presented to you, with every underlying analysis handed over. Yours to use, cite, and build on.
