The engagement — no surprises

Two cycles a year,
four blocks each

We run two consulting cycles a year: October–January and March–June. Each engagement is scoped to a single cycle — roughly a semester of work by a dedicated team.

Start of cycle

Scoping call

A structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like. One hour, no commitment.

Week 1

Proposal

A written scope you sign off on. What we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end.

Through the cycle

The project

A team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated Project Manager. A mid-point review where you course-correct, and working sessions as the analysis firms up. The block is big because this is where the work lives.

End of cycle

Board-ready presentation

Presented to you and yours, with every underlying analysis handed over.
