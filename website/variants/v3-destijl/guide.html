Design specification — not client-facing

V3 — De Stijl

Thesis: Dutch modernist — Mondrian-inspired asymmetric grids, primary accents against 180-green, oversized geometric composition. The black line is the design; color is placed, not sprayed. One of ten competing design variants; content identical across all ten (see website/variants/content.md).

Design system

Specification

Palette

Off-white #FCFCF7 · Black #161616 · Red #C8102E · Blue #1348A0 · Yellow #F2CB05 · 180-green #00693C as the fourth primary. Color rule (hardened in round 2): the interface color is green only — chips, keys, eyebrow squares are always green; red/blue/yellow live exclusively in mute compositional planes and are never attached to labeled content, so color never fakes a legend. Text on yellow is always black. Body text is black on white, always AA.

Type

Schibsted Grotesk only — 400 body, 500 kickers (uppercase, 0.14–0.16em tracking), 800 display at −0.03em. One family, three voices; the grid does the differentiating.

Line & spacing

The 6px black line divides everything; 2px lines subdivide. No border-radius, no shadows, no gradients anywhere. Compositions are CSS grid with deliberately unequal columns (1.6 / 0.6 / 0.9).

Motion

One motion on the whole site: the 180° numeral makes its half-turn into place on first view (JS-gated, reduced-motion-gated). Generic scroll-reveals were deleted in round 2 — one purposeful movement beats forty polite ones. Hover: cells fill flat color, doors invert to black.

Signature elements

Hero as a Mondrian composition whose cells are content (headline plane, 180° green plane, aphorism on yellow) · door-row CTAs as grid cells · proof strip with colored numerals · team as a mosaic with color swatches · timeline as four proportional week-blocks — the project block is widest because that's where the work lives.

Honesty devices

Missing content sits on yellow plates with black borders — [PLACEHOLDER] as a compositional element. No images at all in this variant: zero raster weight, nothing to fake.

Method & extension

How it was made & how to extend it

Build: hand-written static HTML/CSS, one stylesheet, no framework, no build step, no images. Single font family self-hosted (~138 KB of woff2). The lightest variant of the ten by design.

Mood calibration: Mondrian and Rietveld as *system*, not costume — asymmetric balance, primary color as punctuation, the line as the protagonist. The web translation must resist centering everything; De Stijl balance is asymmetric or it is nothing.

To extend to the full site: every page is a new composition from the same six planes: black line, white ground, four colors. Our Work becomes a gallery of compositions (one per case, Situation/Approach/Outcome as three unequal planes); Impact becomes a proof-strip page; Team gets photo cells (black-and-white, color swatch retained) after the photo session. Never center a composition; never use more than one red plane per viewport; the green plane is always the largest colored plane — brand discipline inside the art discipline.

Deploy: zip-deploy to Netlify via API (see website/variants/tools/deploy.ps1).
