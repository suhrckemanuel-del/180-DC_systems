    Two roles

    Choose the responsibility that fits.

    Cycle 1 recruits in September; Cycle 2 recruits in January/February. Both TU Delft and Erasmus University Rotterdam students are welcome.

    Consultant

    Arrive fresh, learn on the job

    No experience required—and we mean it. You join a trained team, work directly with a real organisation's leadership, and help take a project from research to a board-ready deliverable.

    Your Team Leader owns the overall scope and keeps the team moving; you own the quality of your analysis and contribution.

    Team Leader

    Lead the team through a cycle

    Experience is wanted here. The usual path is one cycle as a Consultant, then stepping up to Team Leader the next.

    You own the project scope, client relationship, team rhythm, and quality of the final recommendation.

    What you learn

    A real client changes how you think.

    Training gives you the methods. The project teaches you when to use them, how to explain them, and what to do when the evidence changes the answer.

    You will practise structured problem-solving, client communication, team delivery, and AI-assisted consulting methods built around traceable evidence rather than confident invention.

    What the application asks for

    Beyond the usual contact and course details, four things. Nothing here needs writing from scratch except the letter.

    • A CVYour most recent one. It does not need rewriting for us.
    • A motivation letterThe only part you write for this application.
    • A grade transcriptWhatever your university issues. We do not expect a particular average.
    • Your LinkedIn URLOptional in practice. An empty profile is not held against you.

    You pick the position you want most, and you can tick to be considered for others. Choosing one does not rule out the rest, so pick the one you actually want.

    How we select

    One screen on paper, then three conversations. Each looks at something different, so a weak stage is not the end of it.

    1. Screen We read the application Every application is read. This is the only stage decided on paper.
    2. Interview 1 Behavioural How you work, how you handle disagreement, and why this rather than the other thing you could be doing.
    3. Interview 2 Read a slide We give you a slide and you tell us what it argues. It tests whether you can find the message in someone else's work, which is most of what consulting reading is.
    4. Interview 3 Case A problem worked out loud with us. We are following how you structure it and what you do when you are unsure, not whether you land on a particular answer.
