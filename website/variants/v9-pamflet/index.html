The manifesto

We believe:

  1. Strategy is not a luxury good.

    The organisations doing the caring deserve the same quality of thinking the profitable ones buy — so we give it away.

  2. Claims need receipts.

    Every deliverable passes an AI-assisted review our branch built and published in the open: a finding must quote the document verbatim, or stay silent. Audit us.

  3. Students are an asset, not an apology.

    Engineers from Delft, economists from Rotterdam — trained, reviewed, and hungrier than any billable hour.

  4. The work speaks, or nothing does.

    No fake logos, no invented statistics, no stock faces on this pamphlet. The gaps are marked until real work fills them.

What we take on — six fronts

The work

  1. Digital Transformation

    The right tools and processes, so a small team stops losing hours to manual work.

  2. Marketing & Engagement

    Reaching the audiences your mission serves, and the donors and volunteers who sustain it.

  3. Financial Sustainability

    Funding models and cost structures that survive beyond the next grant cycle.

  4. Market Assessment

    Evidence on where demand, partners, and competition actually are before you commit resources.

  5. Operational Efficiency

    Finding where effort leaks out of your processes and redesigning them around your capacity.

  6. Impact Measurement

    The numbers that prove your work works, for boards and funders.
