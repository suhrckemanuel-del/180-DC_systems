The engagement — ten to twelve weeks, an exit at every gate

Week by week

Week 0

The call

A structured discovery conversation: your challenge, your constraints, what success looks like. One hour, no commitment.

Week 1

The proposal

A written scope you sign off on — what we will answer, what we won't, and what you'll hold at the end. No surprises mid-project.

Wks 2–11

The project

A team of 4–6 student consultants with a dedicated team lead. A mid-point review where you course-correct. Your time: about an hour a week.

Final wk

The deck

A board-ready presentation, delivered to you and yours, every underlying analysis handed over. Yours to use, cite, and build on.
