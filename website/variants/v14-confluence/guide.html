V14 — Confluence

Thesis: a professional two-city narrative—clean institutional copy beside a contained, credited city film rather than over a full-bleed background. Home moves from real Rotterdam footage through a restrained brand-green confluence into real Delft footage; Mission, For clients, and Students each carry a different route-specific film. It differs from Port's photographic maritime system, Polder's diagrammatic landscape, Lumen's image-light calm, and Atlas's information-first mapping by making the branch's Delft–Rotterdam connection a sequence across pages while keeping motion bounded, optional, and outside the reading layer.

Design system

Specification

PaletteVerified 180DC green #78B038 for surfaces and large decorative marks; AA-safe #4A7322 for links and small green text; extra-safe #3D6B1C for dense labels. White, pale green wash, and green-black neutrals support those three brand greens. No competing hue.
TypographySystem UI stack only—Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial. No font request, no flash, and no font-license or transfer cost. Large editorial headings use tight spacing; body copy stays conventional and calm.
Layout1160px maximum measure, white-dominant split heroes with copy and facts in one column and a contained 8:5 film in the other, then alternating content bands. At 860px the film moves below the complete text block. Cards use 18px corners, hairline green-tinted rules, and restrained elevation.
SignatureFour real, route-specific city films framed as editorial context; visible place labels and documentary credits; a restrained #78B038 grade and two static “confluence” lines inside the media frame; and disciplined fact grids. The green treatment supplies the slight atmospheric quality without fabricating either city.
NavigationOn Home, plain links scroll to What we do (#services), Our work (#work), and Team (#committee). Bordered links with an arrow open Mission, For clients, and Students. On own pages, those scroll links explicitly return to index.html#services, index.html#work, and index.html#committee.
AccessibilitySkip links, visible focus rings, semantic headings, labelled form fields, responsive navigation, AA-safe text colors, descriptive film labels, visible source captions, and 44px playback controls. The text never sits over video. Each source-less video has a poster fallback; reduced motion never attaches the MP4, no-JavaScript renders the poster and full copy, and Data Saver requires an explicit play action.

Real places, visibly credited

Four contained films make the two-city story explicit.

The media is city context, not evidence of 180DC work. Every film sits beside the claim-bearing copy, carries its city in the frame, and names its creator, source, and licence in a visible caption.

01

Home · Rotterdam → Delft

The opening film begins with Rotterdam's river scale, passes through a short #78B038 confluence treatment, and resolves in Delft. Its Rotterdam and Delft excerpts are unique to Home and credited separately; the transition is editorial treatment, not a claim that the two views form one continuous geography.

02

Mission · Delft origin

A separate Oostpoort and water film anchors the branch story where it began. Its caption identifies Delft and explicitly says that the footage is not an 180DC project or team event.

03

For clients · Rotterdam scale

A distinct Maasvlakte film supplies port, water, and infrastructure scale beside the engagement proposition. It is contextual footage—not a client, case study, outcome, or branch engagement.

04

Students · Delft making

A different TU Delft engineering film gives the student page a practical, campus-specific register. The people and work shown belong to the credited source and are never presented as this branch's members or consulting work.

The public pages self-host their compressed MP4s and WebP posters; no third-party player, tracking request, remote embed, or audio stream is shipped. The earlier generated img/hero-confluence.webp remains as historical build material but is no longer referenced by a public page. Its slight fantasy quality survives only as a transparent brand-green grade and static diagonal lines inside each bounded film frame.

Documentary ledger: each public figcaption links to the exact Wikimedia Commons source and its licence. The redesign's final verification must check those links, attribution terms, city identity, file sizes, and the absence of footage reuse across routes before release.

One-shot motion, poster-first delivery

The film is an enhancement, never the hero's foundation.

A

Source-less baseline

Initial HTML stores the route's MP4 path in data-src; the video element has no media source. Its WebP poster, place label, credit, and all hero copy are complete before JavaScript runs. With JavaScript disabled, a matching noscript image fills the same reserved 8:5 frame.

B

One route, one request

media-controller.js attaches only the film present on the visited page, and only when its frame approaches the viewport. Normal-mode playback is muted, inline, and one-shot: it pauses off-screen or when the tab is hidden, exposes a 44px Pause control, stops at the final frame, and offers Replay rather than looping.

C

Preferences are authoritative

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce keeps the MP4 detached and the poster static, including when the preference changes at runtime. Data Saver and 2G connections retain the poster until the visitor explicitly chooses Play. If loading or autoplay fails, the credited poster remains legible and the controller never hides the copy.

The repository now stores several films, so the relevant performance invariant is the transfer for the page a visitor actually opens. No route references, preloads, or prefetches another route's MP4. Posters target 40 KB or less, and final measured transfer—not nominal bitrate—is the release gate.

Content and honesty

Same facts, no invented proof.

The copy follows the current V2/V11 public pages: founded in 2019; first multi-city branch; TU Delft × Erasmus Rotterdam; six service areas; two consulting cycles, October–January and March–June; recruitment in September and January/February; and exactly two roles, Consultant and Team Leader.

The source brand document contains older terminology that prefers Project Manager. Current V2/V11 guides record the later explicit client override to Team Leader, and the user repeated that requirement for V14. Public pages therefore use Team Leader and contain no Project Manager reference.

Real footage changes the evidence standard, not the branch facts. Place labels and source captions say only what the linked documentary source supports. Every caption separates city or campus context from 180DC projects, clients, members, and events; no film may be used to fill a missing case study, team portrait, testimonial, partner, or impact result.

Visible placeholder register

Impact numbers; three case studies with Situation → Approach → Outcome; team photos; provisional roster; partner logos; non-submitting intake form; recruitment portal; events calendar; and two member testimonials. Those gaps remain visibly reserved. No city film, still frame, fake client, outcome, statistic, person, quote, or partner fills any one of them.

Extension rule

Reuse the system, never the footage.

A new public route may reuse the split hero, fact grids, service list, cards, static green grade, source-less loading controller, and caption pattern. It must receive its own verified footage and lightweight poster, its own visible creator/source/licence credit and “context, not branch work” disclaimer, and its own measured route budget. Never turn the film back into a full-page text background, preload another route's source, reuse a clip to imply a new context, add audio, loop automatically, or let motion carry information that is absent from the poster and copy.
