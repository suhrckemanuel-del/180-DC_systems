# Privacy Policy — 180DC Delft-Rotterdam Mood Board Tool

*Effective 2026-07-08 · Contact: the 180 Degrees Consulting Delft-Rotterdam branch (see [180dc.org/branches/Delft-Rotterdam](https://180dc.org/branches/Delft-Rotterdam))*

This Pinterest app is an internal tool used by the 180 Degrees Consulting Delft-Rotterdam branch — a student-run, non-profit consultancy — to collect design references for building our own branch website.

**What the app does.** It reads boards and pins from **our own Pinterest account only** (the account that authorizes it), via Pinterest's official API, to assemble a design mood board.

**Data we access.** Board names, pin metadata, and pin images from our own curated boards. Nothing else.

**Data we store.** Retrieved board and pin data is stored locally on a branch member's computer for the duration of the website design project, then deleted.

**What we never do.** We do not access other users' data, do not collect personal data from anyone, do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, do not use data for advertising, and do not operate this app for any users other than ourselves.

**Your rights.** This app has no external users, so it processes no third-party personal data. Questions can be directed to the branch via the contact details on our official branch page linked above.
