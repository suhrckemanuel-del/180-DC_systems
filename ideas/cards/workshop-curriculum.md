# AI workshop curriculum for the branch

## The idea in one line
A short, repeatable workshop series that teaches the branch to use the tools and, more importantly, the consulting principles behind them.

## Why it matters
The tools only compound if people understand why a finding is a finding. A reviewer that hands out fixes builds dependence. A workshop that teaches the Pyramid Principle, governing insight and recommendation specificity builds consultants. This is the teaching leg of the mandate, made concrete.

## How it might work
- Session per principle, each anchored on a real anonymized before/after.
- Consultants run the quality reviewer on their own draft, then defend or revise.
- The research base supplies the evidence for why each principle holds.

## Connects to
- Builds on: Quality Reviewer, Research evidence base
- Feeds into: better deliverables, which is what the reviewer measures

## Open question
Live workshop, self-serve module or both, and who owns delivery once we step back.

## Notes
Pairs naturally with the reviewer: the tool is the practice surface, the workshop is the instruction.
