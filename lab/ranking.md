# Ranking and Top-7 Selection

## Method

Each demo scored 1–5 on four axes, equally weighted:

- **Impact** — how much it moves the branch's core problems (client quality, recruitment, engagement, deliverable quality, structure).
- **Feasibility** — can Manuel build a convincing version with Claude + scripts in days, not months, with data the branch actually has.
- **Credibility** — does it survive a skeptical audience; does it follow the branch principles (quote-or-abstain, teach-don't-ghostwrite) so a demo can't be embarrassed by a hallucination.
- **Wow** — does the first demo produce a visible "oh." moment in the room.

## The top 7

Selected on total score, then adjusted for portfolio coverage (the top 7 should span the branch's problem list, not cluster on one category):

1. **[Red-Team CEO](top7/01-red-team-ceo.md)** — deliverable quality / client experience. The strongest demo in the set: near-zero build cost, quote-anchored so it can't bluff, and the demo moment (a team going silent at a question they can't answer) sells itself.
2. **[Discovery-to-Proposal Copilot](top7/02-discovery-to-proposal.md)** — client acquisition. Fixes engagements at the moment of maximum leverage (scoping) and is the existing "client intake assistant" backlog spark, matured.
3. **[Client Meeting Simulator](top7/03-client-meeting-simulator.md)** — training. Rehearsal for the scariest student-consultant moment; the live fishbowl demo is theatrical in the best way.
4. **[Branch Brain](top7/04-branch-brain.md)** — knowledge base. Attacks the deepest structural problem (100% memory turnover); lower feasibility but the payoff compounds every other tool.
5. **[TL Meeting Copilot](top7/05-tl-meeting-copilot.md)** — team lead support. Directly targets "people disliking project teams or team leads," the branch's top retention risk, at a same-day build cost.
6. **[Case Interview Gym](top7/06-case-interview-gym.md)** — recruitment + training. Levels the recruiting field and doubles as training; strong live demo.
7. **[Kickoff-in-a-Box](top7/07-kickoff-in-a-box.md)** — project management / structure. Raises every project's floor at week 1; 90-second demo from a one-page proposal.

## Near misses and where they went

- **Evidence Auditor (17)** and **Chart Doctor (16)** scored top-7 totals but are, honestly, *lenses for Reviewer V2* rather than standalone tools — V2's spec already contemplates specialist lenses. Folding them in beats building them separately. Recommended: add both to the V2 lens roadmap.
- **Workshop Forge (15)** feeds the existing [workshop-curriculum card](../ideas/cards/workshop-curriculum.md) rather than competing with the top 7; it is also the engine that produces the workshop exercises in each top-7 pack.
- **Storyline Trainer (15)** is the best 8th idea and the natural first drill inside Consulting Dojo; park it as Dojo content, not a separate tool.
- **Team Matchmaker (11)** has real impact but the credibility risk (optimization mystique over people decisions) makes it a bad *early* demo. Revisit after trust in the lab is established.
