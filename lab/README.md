One sitting's output from running the branch as an autonomous AI transformation lab: 25 demo-able AI workflows for a 180 Degrees Consulting branch, ranked, with the top 7 built out to prototype-spec depth.

## Ground rules these ideas inherit

Every idea follows the branch principles in [docs/overview.md](../docs/overview.md):

- **Quote or abstain** — a finding without a verbatim quote does not exist.
- **Teach, do not ghostwrite** — tools surface the gap and the principle; the consultant does the rewrite.
- **Rank, do not reject** — where people are screened, a human draws every cutoff.
- **Deterministic contract** — model returns one structured object; a static template owns the pixels.
- **Honest calibration** — strong work earns a high score, not manufactured findings.

## How this connects to what exists

The branch already shipped a Quality Reviewer, designed an HR Screener, and proved a Deck Transform methodology. The 25 ideas deliberately build *around* those (new users, new moments in the branch lifecycle) rather than re-solving deliverable review. Where an idea overlaps an existing tool, the card says so.
